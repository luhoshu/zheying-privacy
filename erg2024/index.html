應變指南（ERG 2024）技術支援

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 24 日

「應變指南」提供 ERG 2024 危險物質查詢、Guide 應變指引、參考資料及現場地圖輔助功能，核心 ERG 資料可於裝置端離線查閱。

主要功能

  • 危險物質與 UN/NA 編號查詢。
  • ERG 2024 Guide 應變指引與相關參考資料。
  • 中文、英文及中英對照查閱。
  • 收藏與最近瀏覽，資料儲存在使用者裝置。
  • 現場地圖與定位輔助，協助呈現隔離及防護區域。

使用提醒

本 App 為 ERG 2024 資訊的行動查閱與現場輔助工具，並非 U.S. Department of Transportation（U.S. DOT）或 Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration（PHMSA）的官方 App，也不取代現場指揮、機關標準作業程序、專業判斷或官方最新指引。

常見問題

沒有網路可以使用嗎？
核心 ERG 查詢、Guide 與內建參考資料可離線使用；地圖底圖及部分系統地圖功能可能需要網路連線。

為什麼需要定位權限？
當使用者主動使用現場地圖相關功能時，App 會使用裝置定位顯示目前位置及輔助現場判讀。定位資料不會傳送至開發者伺服器。

Emergency Response Guide (ERG 2024) Support

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Emergency Response Guide provides ERG 2024 hazardous materials lookup, response guides, reference materials, and incident map assistance. Core ERG information can be accessed offline on the device.

Main Features

  • Hazardous materials and UN/NA number lookup.
  • ERG 2024 response guides and related reference materials.
  • Chinese, English, and bilingual viewing options.
  • Favorites and recent items stored on the user's device.
  • Incident map and location assistance for visualizing isolation and protective areas.

Important Notice

This app is an independent mobile reference and incident-assistance tool for ERG 2024 information. It is not an official app of the U.S. Department of Transportation (U.S. DOT) or the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA), and it does not replace incident command, agency standard operating procedures, professional judgment, or current official guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the app be used without an internet connection?
Core ERG lookup, Guide content, and built-in reference materials are available offline. Map tiles and certain system map features may require an internet connection.

Why does the app request location permission?
When users choose to use incident map features, the app uses device location to display the current position and assist with incident map reference. Location data is not transmitted to a developer-operated server.
