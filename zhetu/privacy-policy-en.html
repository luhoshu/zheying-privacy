Photo Privacy Mask Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Core photo masking and analysis are processed on the user's device. Photos are not uploaded to a developer server for masking.

Photo Access

Users explicitly choose photos through the system photo picker. When a user chooses to save a processed result to Photos, the app requests permission to add the result. Export creates a new photo and does not overwrite the original.

Data Collection

The current version does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking. The developer does not collect selected photos or mask content through a developer server.

On-device Analysis

Face, text, and license-plate assistance and masking use on-device system capabilities. Automatic detection may miss items, so users should review the entire output before sharing or publishing it.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud services, analytics, advertising, or other data processing, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please use the developer support contact listed on the app's App Store product page.
