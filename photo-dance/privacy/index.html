隱私重點：「相片起舞」在 iPhone 或 iPad 裝置上處理選取的照片、音樂、編輯專案及影片匯出；本 App 不會將這些內容上傳至開發者伺服器，也不使用廣告追蹤或第三方分析服務。

一、照片、音樂與作品

使用者可自行選取照片，以及從檔案 App 或裝置上可存取的音樂資料庫選取音訊，用來製作音樂照片影片或動態幻燈片。App 會將用於專案的媒體副本及編輯設定保存於裝置的 App 儲存空間，以便預覽、編輯、輸出及稍後繼續製作。

照片與音訊的預覽、裁切、特效、運鏡、影片合成和輸出在本機進行，不會傳送給開發者。App 不會為上述功能建立開發者帳戶或要求登入。

二、權限用途

照片：使用者主動選取照片製作影片；只有在使用者選擇「儲存到照片」時，App 才會要求必要的照片新增權限，將輸出的影片存進系統相簿。

音樂資料庫：使用者選擇從音樂資料庫加入歌曲時，系統可能要求媒體資料庫存取權限。僅能使用裝置允許存取且沒有數位版權保護限制的音檔；Apple Music 訂閱串流或受保護音樂不能直接作為匯出影片的音軌。

三、資料蒐集、分享與追蹤

本 App 不設置開發者媒體上傳伺服器，不收集用於廣告的識別資料，不出售照片、音樂或作品資料，也不將作品提供給第三方分析或廣告服務。當使用者自行透過 iOS 分享功能傳送影片至其他服務時，該服務的資料處理依其政策辦理。

四、資料保存與刪除

專案草稿及所需媒體副本保存在裝置的 App 儲存空間。使用者可以在「我的作品」中刪除個別作品或批次刪除；移除 App 通常也會移除其本機資料。已由使用者另行儲存至系統照片圖庫或分享出去的影片，不會因刪除 App 草稿而自動移除。

五、Apple 與其他系統服務

若所選照片或檔案原先存放在 iCloud 或其他由使用者使用的雲端服務，iOS 或對應檔案提供者可能為了提供該檔案而進行下載。這不是由本 App 上傳至開發者伺服器；相關服務依其各自的隱私規範運作。

六、聯絡與政策更新

若對隱私權政策有疑問，請寄送電子郵件至 luhoshu@gmail.com，或前往 使用者支援頁面。若您主動寄送電子郵件，郵件服務商將依其政策處理郵件內容，我們僅將您提供的聯絡內容用於回覆與支援。政策如有變更，將更新本頁與日期。

Privacy at a glance: Photo Dance processes selected photos, audio, projects, and video exports on your iPhone or iPad. The app does not upload these materials to a developer-operated server and does not use ad tracking or third-party analytics.

1. Photos, audio, and projects

You choose the photos and audio files used to create photo music videos or slideshows. Audio may be selected from Files or accessible on-device music. The app stores working copies of selected media and editing settings locally on your device so you can preview, edit, export, and resume your projects.

Previewing, trimming audio, visual effects, camera motion, video rendering, and exporting are performed on-device. No developer account or login is required.

2. Permissions

Photos: You choose the images for your project. The app requests the necessary add-only Photos permission when you choose to save the exported video to your photo library.

Music library: When you select music from your library, iOS may request permission. Only locally accessible, non-DRM-protected music can be used as an export soundtrack. Apple Music subscription streams and protected songs cannot be directly embedded into an exported video.

3. Data collection, sharing, and tracking

Photo Dance does not operate a developer media-upload server, collect advertising identifiers, sell your photos, audio, or projects, or send your projects to third-party advertising or analytics providers. If you choose to share an exported video through iOS to another service, that service's own privacy terms apply.

4. Storage and deletion

Draft projects and working copies of media are stored inside the app on your device. You can delete individual or multiple drafts from My Projects. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data. Videos already saved separately to your Photos library or shared elsewhere are not automatically deleted when you delete a draft.

5. Apple and other system services

If a selected photo or file is stored in iCloud or another cloud-backed file provider, iOS or that provider may download it at your request. This is separate from uploading it to a Photo Dance developer server. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Contact and updates

For privacy questions, email luhoshu@gmail.com or visit Photo Dance Support. If you voluntarily send an email, your mail provider processes it under its own policies; we use the contact details you provide only to respond and assist. We may update this policy, with the latest date posted on this page.
