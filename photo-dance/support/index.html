相片起舞可以做什麼？

使用單張照片搭配音樂、運鏡與視覺特效，或用多張照片製作動態幻燈片，預覽後輸出 MP4 影片。支援繁體中文與英文，iPhone 與 iPad 均可使用。

五步驟開始製作

  1. 選照片：「音樂照片」選一張；「動態幻燈片」可加入多張照片及調整順序。
  2. 選音樂：從檔案或可使用的本機音樂資料庫選取歌曲。可試聽，指定開始與結束位置，調整片段長度。
  3. 選模板：套用柔和或強烈風格，也能自訂運鏡、特效、波形、色彩與文字。
  4. 預覽：播放並確認照片畫面與音樂配合。
  5. 匯出：產生 MP4 影片，再儲存到照片或分享。

比例與構圖

單張「音樂照片」預設照片原始比例，亦可選 9:16、16:9、1:1、4:5、4:3。多張「動態幻燈片」使用統一影片比例（9:16、16:9、1:1、4:5、4:3），每張照片仍可自行調整構圖。

常見問題

為什麼無法加入某首 Apple Music 歌曲？

Apple Music 訂閱或受 DRM 保護的音樂無法直接放進匯出影片。請改用自己有權使用的 MP3、M4A 或 WAV 音檔，透過「檔案」加入。

可以只使用副歌嗎？

可以。先試聽歌曲，在波形上指定起點和終點，再自由縮短或延長選取片段。

作品儲存在哪裡？如何刪除？

草稿保存在 App 裝置本機空間，可從「我的作品」繼續編輯，也能勾選多筆批次刪除。刪除草稿不會刪除相簿中的原始照片。

為什麼無法將影片儲存到照片？

請確認系統已允許本 App 將影片加入照片圖庫，並檢查裝置剩餘空間。若影片很長或效果很多，也可先改用較低輸出畫質測試。

What can Photo Dance do?

Create a music video from one photo with camera motion and visual effects, or turn multiple photos into an animated slideshow. Preview your project, then export an MP4. The app supports English and Traditional Chinese on iPhone and iPad.

Five steps to create a video

  1. Photos: Pick one image for Music Photo or add and reorder multiple images for a Slideshow.
  2. Music: Import an audio file or choose accessible local music. Preview a song, select start and end points, and fine-tune the clip.
  3. Template: Choose a gentle or energetic style. You can fine-tune motion, effects, audio visualization, color, and text.
  4. Preview: Play your video and confirm the visuals and music.
  5. Export: Create an MP4 to save to Photos or share.

Aspect ratios

A single Music Photo defaults to the original photo ratio; other options are 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, and 4:3. Slideshows have a consistent output ratio (9:16, 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, or 4:3), and individual photos can still be reframed.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I use some Apple Music songs?

Apple Music subscription streams and DRM-protected tracks cannot be embedded into exported videos. Import an MP3, M4A, or WAV file you have permission to use through Files instead.

Can I use only the chorus?

Yes. Preview the song and place the start and end markers on the waveform. Adjust either edge to make the clip shorter or longer.

Where are drafts stored, and can I delete them?

Drafts are stored locally in the app. Open My Projects to resume editing or select multiple drafts to delete. Deleting a draft does not remove your original photos from your photo library.

Why can't I save a video to Photos?

Check that the app has permission to add videos to your Photos library and that the device has sufficient free storage. For lengthy projects with many effects, try a lower export quality to troubleshoot.
