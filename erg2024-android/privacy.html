應變指南（ERG 2024）Android 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 26 日

「應變指南」Android 版不要求建立帳號，開發者未建置使用者資料伺服器，不包含廣告、跨 App 追蹤或第三方分析功能。核心 ERG 資料、收藏、最近瀏覽與 App 設定主要於使用者裝置端處理及保存。

資料收集

目前版本不要求使用者提供姓名、電話、電子郵件或其他帳號資料。開發者不透過自有伺服器收集使用者的搜尋紀錄、收藏、最近瀏覽內容、事故位置或定位歷史。

定位資料

當使用者主動使用現場地圖相關功能時，App 可能依 Android 系統權限取得裝置目前位置，用於顯示所在位置及輔助現場地圖判讀。定位資料不會傳送至開發者伺服器，開發者也不建立使用者的位置歷史紀錄。

線上地圖服務

Android 版現場地圖使用內政部國土測繪中心「臺灣通用電子地圖」線上圖資。顯示線上底圖時，App 會直接透過網路向國土測繪中心取得目前畫面所需的地圖圖磚。開發者不經由自有伺服器轉送、儲存或分析這些地圖請求。

若線上底圖無法載入，核心 ERG 查詢資料仍可於裝置端使用；現場防護範圍亦可透過比例示意方式輔助判讀。

本機資料

收藏、最近瀏覽及部分 App 設定儲存在使用者裝置本機。除非使用者自行刪除 App、清除相關資料或由系統移除，這些資料不會主動上傳至開發者。

廣告、分析與追蹤

目前版本不包含廣告 SDK、第三方分析 SDK 或跨 App 追蹤功能，也不會出售或出租使用者資料。

資料來源與免責說明

本 App 以 ERG 2024 相關公開資料為基礎提供查閱與現場輔助功能，並非 U.S. Department of Transportation（U.S. DOT）或 Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration（PHMSA）的官方 App。使用者仍應依官方最新資料、現場指揮、專業判斷及所屬機關作業程序進行應變。

政策變更

若未來版本新增帳號、雲端同步、分析、廣告、AI 或其他會改變資料處理方式的功能，本政策將配合更新。

Emergency Response Guide (ERG 2024) Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The Android version of Emergency Response Guide does not require an account. The developer does not operate a user-data server, and the current version does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics. Core ERG data, favorites, recent items, and app settings are primarily processed and stored on the user's device.

Data Collection

The current version does not require users to provide a name, phone number, email address, or account information. The developer does not collect users' search history, favorites, recent items, incident locations, or location history through a developer-operated server.

Location Data

When users choose to use incident map features, the app may access the device's current location through Android system permissions to display the user's position and assist with incident map reference. Location data is not transmitted to a developer-operated server, and the developer does not create a location history.

Online Map Service

The Android version uses the Taiwan Electronic Map provided by Taiwan's National Land Surveying and Mapping Center (NLSC) as the online incident-map basemap. When the online basemap is displayed, the app directly requests only the map tiles needed for the current view from the NLSC service. The developer does not proxy, store, or analyze those map requests through a developer-operated server.

If the online basemap cannot be loaded, core ERG reference data remains available on the device, and incident protection areas can still be viewed using the app's proportional schematic mode.

Local Data

Favorites, recent items, and certain app settings are stored locally on the user's device. Unless the user deletes the app, clears related data, or the system removes the data, this information is not automatically uploaded to the developer.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The current version does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking, and the developer does not sell or rent user data.

Data Source and Disclaimer

This app provides reference and incident-assistance features based on publicly available ERG 2024-related information. It is not an official app of the U.S. Department of Transportation (U.S. DOT) or the Pipeline and Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (PHMSA). Users should rely on current official information, incident command, professional judgment, and applicable agency procedures when making operational decisions.

Policy Changes

If a future version adds accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, AI, or other features that change how data is handled, this policy will be updated accordingly.
