隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

本政策適用於由 Hoshu Lu 開發的「WEB TOUCH－電視牆觸控」iPad App（以下稱「本 App」）。本 App 讓獲授權的使用者透過區域網路操作相容電視牆設備原有的網頁控制介面。

隱私摘要：本 App 不要求建立開發者帳號，不內建廣告或跨 App 追蹤，也不使用開發者自有伺服器收集設備帳號、密碼或操作畫面。設備連線設定留存在 iPad 本機。登入與控制通訊是與使用者自行連接的設備網頁進行。

一、設備連線設定

為了記住連線目標，本 App 會在 iPad 本機保存由使用者輸入的設備 IP 位址、連接埠、HTTP／HTTPS 方式及登入頁路徑，供後續連線使用。設定資料保存在 App 本機偏好設定，不會由開發者另外上傳至自有伺服器。連線時，系統會依該設定向指定設備提出網路請求。

二、設備帳號、密碼與網站資料

使用者在設備原有網頁輸入帳號、密碼，由該設備網站處理登入與權限驗證。本 App 不會另外讀取、保存或傳送這些帳密給開發者。設備網頁可能使用 Cookie、登入工作階段及其他網站儲存機制，這些資料依 iPadOS WebKit 與設備網頁的行為處理。若啟用原廠網頁的「記住密碼」，請注意共用 iPad 的存取安全。

三、網路通訊與第三方設備

本 App 會載入使用者指定的設備控制網頁，並將操作傳遞給該網頁。所連接設備及其網站可能自行處理登入、操作紀錄或必要的網路通訊；這些行為由設備及供應商決定，本 App 無法代替供應商保證其資料使用方式。

有些設備使用未加密的 HTTP 連線。請只在可信任的區域網路使用設備，並由設備管理者妥善設定帳號與網路權限。如設備支援 HTTPS，可依實際設備設定使用。

四、廣告、分析與資料分享

目前本 App 未整合第三方廣告 SDK、跨 App 追蹤工具或開發者自有的使用行為分析服務。開發者不販售本 App 使用者的設備登入資料。除連線至使用者自行選定的設備與相關網頁運作外，本 App 不以開發者伺服器集中接收或備份設備控制內容。

五、資料管理與刪除

使用者可在 App 的設備設定修改已儲存的連線資訊。若不再使用本 App，可在 iPad 移除 App 以刪除其本機設定；WebKit 網站資料、系統備份及設備端留存的工作階段或紀錄，仍依 iPadOS 與設備系統的管理方式處理，必要時請登出設備並由設備管理者清理。

六、聯絡技術支援與本網站

若使用者主動寄送支援郵件，開發者會取得該郵件所含的寄件地址、問題描述與附件，用於回覆及問題處理。請勿寄送設備密碼、機密畫面或不必要的個人資料。

本網站使用 GitHub Pages 託管，頁面本身未放置廣告、追蹤像素、網站分析腳本或個人資料收集表單；網站託管商可能依其政策處理必要的技術連線紀錄。

七、政策更新與聯絡方式

若本 App 的功能或資料處理方式改變，本政策及更新日期會隨之調整。隱私問題請聯絡 Hoshu Lu：luhoshu@gmail.com。操作問題請至技術支援頁面。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy applies to WEB TOUCH – Video Wall Controller for iPad (the “App”), developed by Hoshu Lu. The App allows authorized users to operate compatible video-wall equipment over a local network through that equipment's existing web control interface.

Privacy summary: No developer account is required. The App contains no ads or cross-app tracking and does not use a developer-operated server to collect equipment login credentials or screen content. Device connection settings stay on the iPad. Sign-in and control traffic are handled through the device website selected by the user.

1. Connection settings

To remember where to connect, the App locally stores the device IP address, port, HTTP/HTTPS scheme, and login-page path entered by the user. This information is stored in the App's on-device preferences and is not separately uploaded to a developer-operated server. Connecting sends requests to the equipment specified by the user.

2. Device credentials and website storage

You enter your username and password directly into the equipment's original web login form. Authentication is handled by that equipment's website. The App does not independently read, store, or send these credentials to the developer. The website may use cookies, sessions, or other website storage managed through iPadOS WebKit and the equipment's own implementation. Exercise caution with the equipment webpage's “remember password” option on shared iPads.

3. Network connections and third-party equipment

The App loads the device control page chosen by the user and passes control interactions to it. The equipment and its web interface may independently process authentication, device logs, and network communications. Such behavior is determined by the equipment and its provider; this App does not claim to control or guarantee those third-party practices.

Some controllers use unencrypted HTTP connections. Use only trusted local networks, restrict access to authorized users, and use HTTPS where supported by the equipment.

4. Ads, analytics, and disclosure

The App does not currently integrate third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app trackers, or developer-operated usage analytics. The developer does not sell equipment credentials. Other than accessing the user's chosen device and supporting its website operations, the App does not send control content to a developer-operated collection or backup server.

5. Managing and deleting data

You can change saved connection details under Device Settings. Removing the App deletes its local app settings. WebKit website data, system backups, and sessions or logs retained by the device itself are handled according to iPadOS and the equipment's management features. Sign out or contact the equipment administrator if device-side cleanup is required.

6. Support requests and this website

If you email support, the developer receives the sender address, message, and attachments you provide for troubleshooting and replying. Do not send equipment passwords, confidential display content, or unnecessary personal information.

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages and contains no ads, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or personal-data submission forms. The hosting provider may maintain necessary technical connection or security logs under its policies.

7. Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when App features or data practices change. Contact Hoshu Lu at luhoshu@gmail.com for privacy questions. See the Support page for assistance.
