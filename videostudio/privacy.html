適用範圍與摘要

本政策適用於呂和樹（Hoshu Lu）開發的「影音工作室」iPhone／iPad App。本 App 將影片剪輯、照片動態製作、音樂編輯、動態片尾及影片語音字幕整合於共同作品中。

媒體與編輯處理在裝置端執行。App 不會主動將你選取的照片、影片、音訊、字幕或參考稿上傳至開發者伺服器或第三方 AI 服務；不需要註冊帳號，也不整合廣告、使用者追蹤或第三方分析服務。

一、處理哪些資料及用途

  • 你主動選取的照片、影片、音樂與片尾背景，用於匯入、預覽、裁切、運鏡、效果、配樂與影片合成。
  • 影片原聲及辨識用音訊，用於產生語音字幕。App 使用 Apple SpeechAnalyzer／SpeechTranscriber；首次使用可能需連線下載 Apple 系統語音資源，功能仍受裝置及語言支援限制。
  • 字幕原文與校正內容、時間碼、樣式、獨立文字、參考稿及常用字彙，用於校對、時間調整、預覽及匯出。
  • 作品名稱、素材副本、照片子專案、配樂與片尾設定、字幕版本、編輯狀態及偏好，用於保存及繼續編輯。

二、儲存、快取與刪除

共同作品、編輯資料及所需媒體副本保存在 App 的本機儲存空間；預覽、音訊分析及匯出也可能產生快取或暫存檔案。

你可從「我的作品」刪除不需要的作品，亦可選取多筆刪除。設定內可清除可重建的預覽快取。符合條件的作品可選擇「只保留文字紀錄」，經確認後移除該作品在 App 內的來源影音副本；此後不能直接重新編輯或匯出該作品影片。

上述操作不會刪除相簿或「檔案」中的原始素材，也不會自動刪除已另存或分享的成品。部分匯出副本可能仍留在各儲存位置，需另行管理。刪除 App 會移除其本機資料；重要作品與成品請另行保存。裝置備份、iCloud 同步及已分享副本依你的系統與服務設定處理。

三、權限用途

  • 照片選取／存取：依你的操作取得要編輯的照片、影片或背景；系統選取器提供你選取的項目。
  • 新增照片：將完成影片儲存至照片圖庫。
  • 音樂資料庫：在你選擇該來源時取得可存取的本機音樂。App 不繞過 DRM，也不提供訂閱歌曲下載或解密。
  • 語音辨識：將既有影片的語音轉換為字幕。
  • 檔案存取：依你的選擇匯入素材、參考稿與字彙，或輸出影片及文字。

相關權限可在系統設定管理。目前版本不提供現場拍攝或錄音，也不要求相機、麥克風、定位或聯絡人權限。

四、分享與系統服務

只有在你主動儲存、匯出或分享時，內容才會交由所選的照片圖庫、檔案提供者、App 或服務處理。若原素材位於 iCloud 或其他雲端檔案提供者，系統可能依你的操作下載。這些服務的傳輸、備份與後續使用依其政策及你的設定辦理。

Apple 的語音資源下載、裝置診斷與系統備份由 Apple 系統及相關設定處理；不應將「裝置端處理」理解為所有系統服務完全不連線。

五、支援郵件與本網站

你主動寄送支援郵件時，開發者會收到寄件地址、內文與附件，僅供問題處理及必要聯繫。請避免傳送私密原始素材、個人資料或機密內容；支援不要求提供完整原始影片。

本網站由 GitHub Pages 託管，未加入廣告、追蹤程式、表單或第三方分析工具。託管服務可能依其政策處理 IP 位址及連線／安全紀錄，這與 App 的本機媒體處理不同。

六、政策更新與聯絡

若功能或資料處理方式改變，會更新本頁及日期。隱私相關問題請聯絡呂和樹（Hoshu Lu）：luhoshu@gmail.com。操作問題請見技術支援。

Scope and overview

This policy applies to Video Studio (影音工作室), an iPhone and iPad app developed by Hoshu Lu. It combines video editing, animated photo sequences, music, credits and speech subtitles within shared projects.

Media processing takes place on your device. The app does not proactively upload selected photos, videos, audio, subtitles or reference scripts to a developer server or third-party AI service. No account is required. The app does not integrate ads, user tracking or third-party analytics.

1. Data used

Selected media and working copies support import, preview, trimming, camera motion, effects, music and rendering. Video audio is used to generate subtitles with Apple's SpeechAnalyzer/SpeechTranscriber. Apple speech resources may need an initial online download; availability depends on the device and language.

Corrected transcripts, timecodes, text layers, styles, scripts, vocabulary, project titles, photo subprojects, editing settings and subtitle versions are stored to support editing and exports.

2. Storage and deletion

Projects and necessary media copies are stored in the app on your device. Preview, analysis and export operations may create caches and temporary files. Delete projects in My Projects or clear rebuildable preview caches in Settings. Eligible projects offer a confirmation-based text-only archive that removes their app-owned source media; those projects can no longer be directly re-edited or exported as videos.

These actions do not delete originals in Photos or Files, or independently saved or shared exports. Other export copies may remain and require separate management. Removing the app removes its local data. Device backups and cloud synchronization depend on your system and service settings; keep separate copies of important work.

3. Permissions

Photo access supports selected photos, videos and backgrounds; add-only access saves finished videos. Music-library access supports accessible local tracks. Speech-recognition permission supports transcription of existing videos. File access supports media, scripts, vocabulary and exports selected by you. Permissions can be managed in system settings. This version does not capture live video or audio and does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions. The app does not bypass DRM or decrypt subscription music.

4. Sharing and system services

When you choose to save or share, your selected destination handles the content under its own policies. Cloud-backed media may be downloaded by iOS or a file provider. Apple speech-resource downloads, diagnostics, backups and cloud services follow their respective policies and settings. On-device processing does not mean that every system service is offline.

5. Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and attachments only for support and necessary follow-up. Do not send confidential or sensitive original media; full original videos are not required. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages without added advertising, tracking scripts, forms or third-party analytics. The hosting provider may process connection and security logs, including IP addresses, under its policies.

6. Contact and updates

Policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Contact Hoshu Lu at luhoshu@gmail.com, or visit Support.
