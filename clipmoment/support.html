使用支援

隨手剪提供影片分割、合併、時間軸剪輯、背景音樂編輯、逐段原聲音量調整、動態片尾及作品儲存。

如何分割影片？

左右移動時間軸，讓需要分割的位置對準播放游標，選取影片片段後按「分割」。

如何分割背景音樂？

展開音樂時間軸，左右移動時間軸，將要分割的位置對準紅色播放游標，選取音樂片段後按「分割」。拖動片段中央可移動整段音樂；拖動兩端把手可裁短音樂。

如何調整原始聲音？

選取影片片段後調整該段原聲音量；背景音樂的音量可另外調整。

如何繼續編輯？

在首頁開啟「我的作品」，選取已儲存的專案即可繼續。

為什麼某些 Apple Music 歌曲不能匯入？

受數位版權保護的串流歌曲可能無法作為可編輯音訊匯入。請使用可合法使用且可存取的音訊檔案。

Support

ClipMoment offers video splitting and merging, timeline editing, background music editing, per-clip original audio controls, scrolling end credits, and saved projects.

How do I split a video?

Move the timeline until the desired position aligns with the playhead. Select the video clip and tap Split.

How do I split music?

Expand the music timeline, move the timeline until the desired position aligns with the red playhead, select the music segment, and tap Split. Drag the middle to move a segment; drag its edge handles to trim it.

How do I adjust original audio?

Select a video clip and adjust its original audio volume. Background music volume can be controlled separately.

How do I resume editing?

Open My Projects on the home screen and select a saved project.

Why can't I import certain Apple Music tracks?

DRM-protected streaming music may not be available as editable audio. Use accessible audio files that you are permitted to use.
