隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

本政策適用於「聽寫譯通」iPhone 與 iPad App（以下稱「本 App」），由呂和樹（Hoshu Lu）開發。本 App 提供錄音、音訊或影片匯入、即時聽寫、即時翻譯、雙向口譯、同聲口譯，以及即時翻譯頁面中的照片翻譯。

隱私重點：本 App 不要求註冊帳號，沒有開發者自有伺服器用來接收或保存你的錄音、逐字稿、翻譯文字或照片，也不使用廣告追蹤功能。文字、照片與音訊的處理會使用裝置及 Apple 提供的系統框架；部分功能可能需要下載 Apple 語言資源。

一、處理的內容與用途

當你使用相關功能時，本 App 會依你的操作處理麥克風收音、錄音檔案、選取的音訊或影片、辨識後文字、翻譯內容及你主動選取或拍攝的照片。這些內容僅用於 App 所提供的轉寫、校正、翻譯、口譯、檢視、儲存與分享功能。

照片翻譯是在拍照完成或從相簿選取照片後才進行文字辨識；不會持續分析相機預覽影像。翻譯來源語言由你手動指定。拍攝或選取的照片，只有在你主動按下「儲存照片翻譯紀錄」時，才會連同原文與譯文對照存入本機資料庫。

二、Apple 系統語音、翻譯與裝置端功能

本 App 使用 Apple 的語音辨識、翻譯、文字辨識及裝置端文字處理相關技術，例如 Speech、Translation、Vision 與支援裝置上的系統模型。辨識及翻譯功能是否可用，取決於裝置、系統版本、語言和已安裝的語言資源；首次使用或下載語言資源時可能需要網路連線。

本 App 不會自行將你的錄音、逐字稿、照片或翻譯內容上傳至開發者自有伺服器。Apple 系統服務、語言資源下載、裝置備份及你所選擇的其他服務，依 Apple 或相應服務提供者的政策與系統設定處理。

三、本機儲存與資料刪除

依你的操作，本 App 可在裝置儲存會議錄音、匯入媒體的本機副本、逐字稿、即時聽寫及口譯紀錄、翻譯內容、照片翻譯的照片與文字對照、常用字彙及使用偏好。即時模式的原始音訊是否保留，依功能及你的選擇而定。

你可使用 App 的「資料庫」檢視及刪除相關紀錄；刪除照片翻譯紀錄時，也會移除由本 App 保存的該筆照片副本。已透過分享功能匯出至照片、檔案、iCloud 或其他 App 的副本，必須到其儲存位置另行管理。移除 App 可移除其本機資料；系統備份及其他儲存位置的副本，仍依你的系統設定處理。

四、權限與檔案存取

  • 麥克風：依你的操作進行錄音、即時聽寫與口譯。
  • 語音辨識：將錄音、所選音訊或現場收音轉換成文字。
  • 相機：你選擇「拍照翻譯」時，拍攝需要翻譯的文字。
  • 照片選取：透過系統選取器，只讀取你主動選擇的照片或媒體。
  • 檔案：依你的選擇匯入音訊、影片，或匯出文字與其他內容。

你可透過 iOS／iPadOS 的「設定」管理相關權限。拒絕特定權限可能使對應功能無法使用，但不代表所有功能都無法使用。

五、帳號、廣告、分析及資料分享

本 App 不要求建立帳號，不內建第三方廣告追蹤或跨 App 追蹤機制，亦未整合開發者部署的使用者行為分析服務。開發者不會出售或出租使用者錄音、逐字稿、照片或翻譯內容。

只有在你主動使用分享或匯出功能時，內容才會交給你選擇的 App、檔案儲存位置或外部服務，其後續使用依該服務的隱私政策與你的設定處理。

六、技術支援與本網站

若你主動寄送支援郵件，開發者會收到你提供的寄件地址、問題內容及附件，用於處理問題與必要回覆。請勿寄送不必要的個人資料、機密錄音或含有敏感資訊的原始照片。

本頁由 GitHub Pages 託管，頁面沒有廣告、分析程式、追蹤像素或資料收集表單。網站託管商可能依其政策保有必要的連線或安全紀錄。

七、政策更新與聯絡

若 App 功能或資料處理方式有所變更，會更新本政策及日期。隱私問題可聯絡開發者呂和樹（Hoshu Lu）：luhoshu@gmail.com。使用支援請參閱技術支援頁面。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy applies to Ting Xie Yi Tong (the “App”), an iPhone and iPad app developed by Hoshu Lu. The App provides recording, audio/video import, live dictation, live translation, two-way interpreting, continuous interpreting, and photo translation available from the live translation screen.

Privacy overview: No account is required. The App has no developer-operated server for receiving or storing your recordings, transcripts, translations, or photographs and does not use advertising trackers. Processing relies on device functionality and Apple system frameworks; Apple language resources may need to be downloaded.

1. Information processed

When you use the relevant features, the App processes microphone audio, recorded audio, imported audio/video, recognized text, translations, and photographs you choose or take. These are used to transcribe, edit, translate, interpret, view, save and share content at your direction.

Photo text recognition takes place after you capture or choose a still image, not through continuous live camera scanning. You select the source language yourself. A photo and its paired original/translated text are saved in the local history only when you select the option to save a photo translation record.

2. Apple system features and language resources

The App uses Apple speech recognition, translation, text recognition and on-device text processing technologies, including frameworks such as Speech, Translation and Vision, and system models on supported devices. Availability varies by device, operating system, language and installed language resources. An Internet connection may be required to download language resources or prepare certain system features.

The App does not itself send your recordings, transcripts, photographs or translations to a developer-operated server. Apple system services, resource downloads, device backups and any services you choose are governed by the applicable provider policies and your system settings.

3. Local storage and deletion

Depending on your actions, the App may save recordings, local copies of imported media, transcripts, dictation and interpreting history, translations, photographs and paired translation text, custom vocabulary, and preferences on your device. Whether original audio from live sessions is retained depends on the feature and your choices.

You can view and delete relevant records in the App's Database. Deleting a saved photo translation also removes the photo copy stored by this App. Copies that you previously exported or shared to Photos, Files, iCloud or another app must be managed separately. Deleting the App removes its local app data, but backups and copies stored elsewhere depend on your device and provider settings.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: for recordings, live dictation and interpreting you initiate.
  • Speech Recognition: to transcribe recordings, selected audio and live speech.
  • Camera: to capture still photographs for photo translation when selected.
  • Photo selection: to access items you explicitly select using the system picker.
  • Files: to import user-selected audio/video and export content.

You can manage permissions in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Denying a permission may disable only the functions that depend on it.

5. Accounts, advertising, analytics and sharing

No account is required. The App does not integrate third-party advertising tracking, cross-app tracking or developer-operated behavioral analytics. The developer does not sell or rent your recordings, transcripts, photos or translation content.

Content is passed to a destination you choose only when you initiate export or sharing. The recipient app or service processes it under its own privacy policy and your settings.

6. Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message and attachments you provide for troubleshooting and responding. Do not send unnecessary personal information, confidential recordings or sensitive original photographs.

This page is hosted by GitHub Pages and has no advertisements, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or forms. The hosting provider may process technical connection or security logs under its policies.

7. Updates and contact

This policy may be updated if the App's functions or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact Hoshu Lu at luhoshu@gmail.com. For assistance, see the Support page.
