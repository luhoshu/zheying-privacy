使用支援

「聽寫譯通」將錄音轉寫、即時聽寫、語言翻譯與口譯整合在同一支 App，提供 iPhone 與 iPad 使用。

系統需求：iOS／iPadOS 26.0 或更新版本。實際語音辨識、翻譯與系統模型支援情況依裝置、語言及 Apple 已安裝資源而異；首次使用可能須連網下載語言資源。

首頁六項功能

① 開始錄音

錄製會議或訪談，保存錄音，必要時再進行逐字稿辨識。

② 匯入檔案

從「檔案」匯入音訊或影片，將語音轉成可閱讀、校正與分享的文字。

③ 即時聽寫

將現場說話內容轉為文字，方便查看、修正及保存。

④ 即時翻譯

自行指定來源與目標語言，支援繁體中文、英文、日文、韓文之間的翻譯；在此頁底部「開始」右側可開啟「照片翻譯」。

⑤ 雙向口譯

適合中英文雙方輪流對話，可進行語音辨識、翻譯與語音播放。

⑥ 同聲口譯

依所選語言方向持續辨識及翻譯，支援中、英、日、韓四種語言的相關翻譯方向。

照片翻譯如何使用？

  1. 首頁選擇「即時翻譯」，指定原文與譯文語言。
  2. 在畫面底部「開始」右側點選「照片翻譯」。
  3. 選擇「拍照翻譯」或「相簿翻譯」。拍照後才進行文字辨識，不提供相機即時翻譯。
  4. 查看逐項排列的原文與譯文；菜單等資料可逐項核對，並可修正辨識的原文後重新翻譯。
  5. 需要保留時，點選「儲存照片翻譯紀錄」，再到下方「資料庫」查看。

常見問題

第一次翻譯顯示等待或尚未準備好？
Apple 的語音辨識或翻譯語言資源可能尚未下載。請確認裝置網路與系統支援情況，依提示完成資源準備；如果畫面顯示可重試，請按「重試翻譯」。

為什麼有些語言或裝置無法使用？
可用的語音辨識、翻譯與語音朗讀功能會受到系統版本、裝置硬體及 Apple 語言資源影響。請更新系統並確認所選語言可用。

照片翻譯會自動保存嗎？
不會。請在翻譯完成後主動按「儲存照片翻譯紀錄」，才會寫入本機資料庫。

如何查看或刪除紀錄？
在首頁底部點「資料庫」，依類別尋找錄音、逐字稿、口譯或照片翻譯紀錄。刪除 App 內紀錄不會自動刪除先前匯出到其他位置的副本。

辨識或翻譯有錯誤怎麼辦？
背景噪音、說話速度、口音、拍攝角度與文字清晰度都可能影響結果。重要內容請自行核對；照片翻譯可修正原文並重新翻譯。

是否一定要連網？
部分功能使用 Apple 裝置端資源；首次下載語言資料或準備系統模型可能需要連線。不能保證所有語言、所有裝置在完全離線下都能執行每項功能。

Support

Ting Xie Yi Tong combines recording, speech-to-text, live dictation, translation and interpreting on iPhone and iPad.

Requirements: iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later. Available speech, translation and system model features vary by device, language and installed Apple language resources. A connection may be required to download resources for first use.

Six main features

1. Record Audio

Record meetings or interviews and transcribe saved recordings when needed.

2. Import Files

Import selected audio or video files to produce text you can review, edit and share.

3. Live Dictation

Turn speech into text while listening, with options to review and save records.

4. Live Translation

Choose source and target languages among Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean. Photo Translation is beside Start at the bottom of this screen.

5. Two-way Interpreting

Translate alternating conversations between Traditional Chinese and English, with spoken output.

6. Continuous Interpreting

Continuous speech recognition and translation using supported directions among the four languages.

Photo translation

  1. Open Live Translation from the home screen and choose your source and target languages.
  2. Tap Photo Translation beside Start at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Take a still photo or choose an image from Photos. The App does not continuously translate a live camera feed.
  4. Review the paired original and translated text. You can correct recognized source text and translate again.
  5. Tap Save Photo Translation Record when you want to keep the image and paired text in the local Database.

FAQ

Why does first use take longer?
Some Apple speech and translation resources must be installed or prepared first. Check connectivity and language availability, follow any system prompts, and use Retry Translation if offered.

Do photo translations save automatically?
No. Save the result explicitly, then find it under Database → Photo Translation.

Where are my records?
Open the Database tab to find saved recordings, transcripts, interpretation history and saved photo translations. Copies exported elsewhere must be managed separately.

Is everything available offline?
Not necessarily. Supported on-device features may work once the required Apple resources are installed, but availability varies by language, operating system and device.

How can I improve accuracy?
Reduce background noise and check accents or unclear pronunciation. For photos, use a sharp image with readable text. Please verify important translation results yourself.
